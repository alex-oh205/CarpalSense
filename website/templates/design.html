How To Use CarpalSense


1.

Place the EMG sensor on your forearm using the band attached to it. Ensure that the cable is facing away from you and that the EMG is placed on the Flexor Pollicis Longus muscle.

2.

Connect a Micro-B to USB cable to the Arduino Nano 33 IOT (located underneath the housing) but do not plug the USB end into the device being connected.

3.

Gently pull the glove over your left hand. Make sure that the Arduino 33 Nano IOT and the rest of the circuit are facing up when you do so. Double check all sensor positions before beginning any data collection.

4.

Plug in the USB into the computer and place your gloved hand flat on a desk in a flat position for 5-8 seconds. Holding this position will allow the sensor to calibrate the EMG.

5.

Once your device is connected, go to your Dashboard and start a new monitoring session. Click Start to begin collecting data. To toggle which sensor you see, use the sensor view menu.

6.

If the accumulated strain on the wrist reaches a “danger zone”, a notification will pop up in the Alerts panel telling you what the risk is and what you should do.



FAQ


Why are my EMG readings off?

Place your hand with the glove on a table flat for about five to eight seconds. If you keep your hand still for this amount of time, the EMG can calibrate and give out accurate readings. Make sure the sensor is plugged in!

Who is this device designed for?

This device is designed for people who frequently use their wrist during their daily lives. Keeping your wrist in a bent position can lead to carpal tunnel syndrome, and this sensor prevents this by giving accurate readings and risk levels of the condition.

How does the device work?

The product uses two sensors, a flex sensor and EMG sensor. The flex sensor detects wrist bending while the EMG sensor detects electrical activity within muscles. Both these sensors work in tandem to give the user accurate readings and a risk level.

Will this be uncomfortable to wear?

This wearable sensor will be perfectly comfortable to wear. It features a comfortable glove that fits well and is easy to put on. We make sure that even with all these wires and other components, the user's quality of life experience isn't diminished in any way.
