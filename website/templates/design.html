The Model



Our Work

The CO₂ Sleeve is a compact device designed to help everyday people reduce carbon dioxide emissions in a simple and safe way, without affecting engine performance or other equipment. It consists of a hollow sleeve with a carefully designed internal structure, including a honeycomb-like pattern that maximizes surface area for CO₂ capture. The cylindrical honeycomb cartridge is easily replaceable when needed. Inside the sleeve, we use an amine-based sorbent, a chemical material known for its ability to selectively absorb CO₂ from the air. Air containing carbon dioxide passes through the sleeve, where the sorbent captures and retains the gas, allowing users to reduce emissions in small, practical ways. The sleeve is modular and easy to install, and its design is focused on functionality, efficiency, and usability.

Software and Methods

We used Autodesk Fusion 360 to design the sleeve and honeycomb structure. The STL model below represents the prototype. By combining CAD design with our amine-based sorbent, we created a modular system that’s easy for everyday users to implement, demonstrating a simple but effective method to reduce CO₂ emissions.
