Sensor Design









Materials

Our sensor used many different parts, and they all work in tandem to deliver accurate readings and provide the user a comfortable experience:

  • A flew and EMG sensor to collect data
  • An arduino to process and deliver the readings to the database
  • A glove to serve as a comfortable way for the user to use the device

A list of materials is shown below. It feautures the different parts used, the quantity / cost of each part, and the purpose each part serves.



Material Quantity Cost Purpose
Arduino Nano 33 IoT 1 $13.00 Processes sensor readings and sends them to the database
Fingerless Glove 1 $13.95 A comfortable sleeve for the user to wear
Flex Sensor 1 $7.95 Measures wrist bending / flexion
OYMotion EMG Sensor 1 $49.50 Measures electrical activity from muscle contractions at the wrist from the skin surface
Clincher Connector 1 $2.95 Connects the flex sensor to the circuit
Elastic Fabric 1 $5.49 Houses the arduino components, flex sensor, and EMG sensor
