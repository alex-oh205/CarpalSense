About Us



Our Team

We are Team CarpalSense, a group of juniors from High Technology High School in Lincroft, New Jersey. We are committed to helping people every day, so we have designed a sensor that monitors wrist position and movement to predict the onset of carpal tunnel syndrome. We believe that this sensor will allow many people to face one less difficulty in their lives.
