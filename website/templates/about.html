About Us



Our Team

We are Team CarpalSense, a group of juniors from High Technology High School in Lincroft, New Jersey. We wanted to help people in work environments requiring prolonged hand and wrist strain from repetitive motions like typing, leading to an increased risk for developing carpal tunnel syndrome. Therefore, we designed a sensor that would allow them to better manage this condition and prevent its further progression. We hope that people who use our device can improve their hand health by knowing how to adjust their posture and when to take breaks.
